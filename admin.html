How this works

  1. Make your changes on any tab. Nothing is public until you press Save & publish (top right).
  2. New and edited listings appear on the home page within about a minute. The individual property pages that Google reads are rebuilt automatically if a Vercel deploy hook is set (see SETUP.md, step 6) – otherwise they update on your next deploy. Either way, visitors always see your latest details.
  3. Photos upload straight to your Cloudinary account and are shrunk first so they load fast on phones. The first photo is the cover.
  4. Untick Show on website to hide a listing without deleting it (handy once it’s sold or let).
  5. The Team and Client reviews sections on the home page stay hidden until you add someone here. Please only add reviews from real clients, with their permission.

Tips

  • Prices are plain numbers (e.g. 85000000). Lettings show “/ month” automatically.
  • Write the description for a real person: street, nearby landmarks, what makes it special. Blank line = new paragraph.
  • If you see “Session expired”, just sign in again – your unsaved edits stay on the page.
